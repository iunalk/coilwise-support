Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 October 2026

Short version: Coilwise has no account and collects no data. Your jobs, readings, photos and signatures are stored only on your device. We never see them.

1. Who we are

Coilwise is developed by İsmet Ünal, an individual developer. Contact: ismet.unal@icloud.com.

2. Data we collect

None. Coilwise has no account or sign-in, and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. The app does not send your data to us or to any server. The only network connection it makes is to the App Store or Google Play to handle purchases.

3. Data stored on your device

Your settings, company details, jobs, readings, photos and signatures are stored only on your device, in the app's own storage. Device backups you enable (iCloud or Google) are handled by Apple or Google under their own terms.

4. Your customers' data

Jobs can contain information about your customers, such as a name, address or signature. You enter and control this information, and you (the technician or company) are responsible for handling it lawfully, for example when you share a report. We have no access to it.

5. Camera and photos

The camera and photo library are used only when you add a photo to a job. When a photo is added, Coilwise re-encodes it and removes its EXIF metadata, including GPS location, device and date information. A company logo you add is re-encoded the same way. On iOS and Android the system photo picker is used, so the app does not get access to your whole library.

6. Reports and backups

PDF reports and backup files are created on your device. They leave it only when you choose to share, print or save them, and they go only where you send them, for example a folder, an email or a messaging app you pick.

7. Purchases

Coilwise Pro is sold and processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never receive your name, payment card or billing details. The app only receives confirmation from the store whether a subscription is active.

8. Deleting your data

You can delete everything at any time in Settings → Delete my data, or by uninstalling the app. You can also delete a single job and its photos and signatures from the job screen.

9. Children

Coilwise is a professional tool and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

10. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is usually nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. You still have rights under applicable law, including the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK), the UK GDPR and US state privacy laws. We do not sell or share personal information. To ask a question, email ismet.unal@icloud.com. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.

12. Contact
