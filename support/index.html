Frequently asked questions

How is the target range chosen?

For TXV/EEV systems, enter the manufacturer's target subcool from the data plate; if you don't, Coilwise shows a typical range and says so. For fixed-orifice systems, the target superheat comes from indoor wet-bulb and outdoor dry-bulb temperatures. The manufacturer's data always takes priority.

Why does it say a fixed-orifice system can't be checked?

When the target superheat is very low (for example, a cool, humid indoor coil on a hot day), a fixed-orifice system can't be charged reliably by superheat. Coilwise tells you not to charge under those conditions. Come back when conditions change or follow the manufacturer's procedure.

How do I back up and restore my jobs?

In Settings → Backup, choose Back up jobs. Coilwise creates a .coilwise file that you save where you like, such as Files, a drive or an email to yourself. To restore it, choose Restore from backup and pick the file. Restoring replaces the jobs on this device. Restoring is always free; making a backup needs Pro.

How do I cancel or restore my subscription?

Cancel in your device's subscription settings: on iPhone, Settings → your name → Subscriptions; on Android, Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. On a new device, open the Pro screen and tap Restore purchases while signed in to the same store account.

Can I use °C and bar, or correct for altitude?

Yes. Choose °F/psi or °C/bar in Settings at any time. Gauge pressures can be corrected for altitude in Settings so saturation temperatures stay accurate above sea level.

Where is my data?

Only on your device. We never see it. See the Privacy Policy.
