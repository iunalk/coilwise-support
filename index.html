Charge checks you can explain

Coilwise turns your pressure and line-temperature readings into superheat and subcool, a plain-language reading of the charge, and a PDF service report for your customer.

  • Superheat & subcoolSaturation data for R-410A, R-454B, R-32, R-22, R-134a, R-404A, R-407C and R-290, built into the app. Dew point for superheat, bubble point for subcool.
  • Charge readingCompares your result with a target range for fixed-orifice and TXV/EEV systems and explains what it suggests.
  • PT chartPressure–temperature lookup in °F/psi or °C/bar, with altitude correction.
  • Jobs and reports ProKeep readings, photos and signatures per job and share a PDF report with your company details.

The calculator, the charge reading and the PT chart are free. Jobs, reports, photos, signatures and backup are part of Coilwise Pro, with a free trial. Prices are shown in the app and on the store page.

Results are estimates. Coilwise is a field aid, not a diagnosis. Follow the equipment manufacturer's data and local codes.
